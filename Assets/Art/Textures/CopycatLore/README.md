# Copycat: the pupil who stayed

Six optional environmental pictures, created with the built-in image generation tool on 29 September 2026. They use the existing Copycat sprite as their identity reference. No new dialogue, objectives or encounter rules.

## Story
Copycat found a friend, Ellie, and copied her in an attempt to belong. The copying became unsettling: identical mistakes, then movement only when nobody watched. The caretaker rewarded her for revealing hiding pupils. Being remembered became more valuable to her than friendship. Her future drawing promises to stay at school; a class picture dated 1986 shows the same unchanged girl. Her original name, what happened to Ellie, and whether she is supernatural remain deliberately unresolved.

The pictures imply this story; they are not six numbered quest steps. Players may encounter them in any order. The 1986 picture is the strongest reveal. Copycat keeps her existing short black haircut, enormous eyes, green jumper and shorts. Do not replace her with a generic long-haired girl.

## Prompts
### 01_AFriend
Landscape 3:2. Heading MY NEW FRIEND. School playground pupil drawing: a cheerful brown-haired pupil offers half a sandwich to Copycat sitting alone on a bench. Copycat watches the friend's face with hopeful huge eyes, carefully mirroring the friend's raised hand. Bottom child's handwriting: 'She likes everything I like.' Warm innocent mood.

### 02_Copying
Landscape 3:2. Heading ART CLUB. Two pinned pupil drawings side by side, both EXACTLY the same crooked sunflower, little blue bird and red house, even same accidental paint splodge. At bottom small scene of brown-haired pupil drawing while Copycat watches the pencil and copies every motion. First drawing signed 'ELLIE'; second signature space rubbed out. Bottom caption: 'Even my mistakes.'

### 03_Still
Portrait 2:3. Heading PLAYTIME. Children's crayon scene of school garden and game of statues. Three normal children laughing and moving, Copycat standing absolutely still with huge staring eyes facing viewer near brick wall, her school shoes surrounded by many dark pencil footprints that stop there. At bottom handwriting: 'She only moves when we turn away.' Include cheerful flowers and football.

### 04_GoodHelper
Portrait 2:3. Heading A GOOD HELPER. Child's drawing: tall lanky stern caretaker in long teal coat with keyring bends to give a large gold star to Copycat. She points down corridor toward tiny hiding schoolchildren. Her face pleased to finally receive praise. Simple navy outlines and crayon. Two-line bottom handwritten caption: 'I told him where they were.' 'He remembered me.' No horror gore.

### 05_Staying
Portrait 2:3. Heading WHEN I GROW UP. Three pupil drawings pinned onto cream paper: small upper sketches adult astronaut and baker. Large main drawing by Copycat shows herself exactly unchanged, standing alone outside same red-brick British primary school. Short handwritten sentence below main picture: 'I am staying here.' Blank Name line. Several faint erased names underneath, illegible.

### 06_Then
Landscape 3:2. Heading OUR SCHOOL IN 1986. Faded pupil-made historical class picture: two rows of eight diverse schoolchildren, teacher, red brick school backdrop, dated 1986 prominently and accurately on small paper strip. Copycat is one child in back row centre, EXACT same face haircut and clothing as reference, eyes staring forward. Other children have simpler ordinary small eyes. Old yellowed paper, worn corners, pencil rubs. Class name labels below drawn pupils; Copycat's label conspicuously blank. Quiet subtle reveal, no explanatory caption.
