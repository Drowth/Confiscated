# Opening diary comic

A new school day now opens with a 25-second illustrated diary page before the
existing live classroom phone incident. Its four panels show Smith's yo-yo,
handheld game, skateboard and robot being confiscated. The last caption gives
Smith's reason to recover them: the caretaker would hold them until the end of
term, and Smith wants them back before going home. Mr Reed taking the phone in
the classroom becomes the fifth confiscation.

The cutscene advances itself, or the player can advance a panel with Space,
Enter, a click, or gamepad South. Holding Escape or gamepad East skips it. A
chase retry bypasses the diary and still starts with the established classroom
retry flow. The artwork is a single generated pencil-and-crayon sheet at
`Assets/Resources/Art/OpeningDiaryComic.png`; captions remain text in the game
so they can be revised and localized without editing the image.
